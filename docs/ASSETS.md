The built-in image-generation tool created the banner and 16 catalogue concepts. All photographs are AI-generated illustrative concepts, not evidence of actual packaging, manufacturing, ingredients or product appearance. Replace or approve them against real goods before launch. ## Prompt set

Banner: premium editorial product mockup of a glass jar of golden cow ghee with a brushed gold lid and cream Maithilok / Cow Ghee label, limestone plinth, brass bowl and spoon, natural linen, warm ivory daylight and forest-green shadow. No official claims, seals, certifications, or mandatory label fabrication.

Each catalogue asset was generated separately using this shared prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Square premium ecommerce studio photograph of [subject]. Warm ivory seamless background, limestone surface, soft daylight from upper left, subtle realistic shadow. Minimal tasteful composition, central subject fills 70 percent, exceptionally realistic food textures. Original illustrative concept for Indian food brand Maithilok. No extra text, logos, certification, official claims, seals or fabricated mandatory labels. Consistent premium photography for a food catalogue. Not a collage.

Subjects: cow ghee 500g, cow ghee 1kg (cream labels, forest-green type); buffalo ghee 500g, buffalo ghee 1kg (cream labels, burgundy type); makhana, thekua, sattu, mango pickle, papad, chura, tilkut, lai, khaja, traditional sweets, traditional snacks, and a forest-green gift hamper. Jar prompts explicitly requested the English brand/product name and the relevant weight.

Admin → Product images supports PNG/JPEG/WebP uploads without code changes. Removing an image from a gallery preserves the file. Uploaded filenames are random and file signatures are inspected. Uploads are private-admin-only, under 5 MB, and SVG uploads are rejected to avoid active content. Local uploads require persistent storage when deploying.
